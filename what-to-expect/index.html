What to expect on your first visit

At Anthony Medical & Chiropractic Center, we take a new approach to providing the care you need. This means we are able to provide you with a wider variety of services in West Georgetown TX than other healthcare offices in the region. To ensure we are meeting all of your wellness needs, we assign a case manager specifically focused on your care. We also understand your time is valuable, so efficient care is just as important as the quality care we provide.

  1. ExpectationWe understand that your healthcare expectations are high and they should be. You are the only person who truly understands your discomfort, pain, and how it affects your daily life. That is why it is important for us to communicate what you can expect on your first visit with us. You need to understand the process prior to your first visit, not after.
  2. CommunicationWe believe it is important for us to always communicate with you in a manner that positions your care goals at the center of all we do. From our first confirmation call and text throughout your care, we focus on clarity and understanding in a way most offices simply do not.
  3. DedicationWhen you become a patient at any of our offices, you become part of an expanded Anthony Chiro family, and we take care of family. We understand some health concerns are resolved quickly and others take a longer journey of care. That is why we are dedicated to assuring you receive the appropriate care needed for your health, no more and no less.
  4. CollaborationWe are a team of healthcare professionals committed to returning you to the highest level of health available to you. This means we work together with you to decide the appropriate care and treatments needed to return you to optimal health. We discuss all your options with you in a clear and concise manner so you can understand how they will have a positive impact on your ability to live life to its fullest. Ultimately, the decision is yours.
  5. AppreciationWe appreciate the confidence you have in our team to care for your healthcare needs. We will never take this lightly or for granted. We will spend each day committed to providing you with the care you need to get the outcomes you expect and deserve.

Meet the team

The team at our West Georgetown office. Names, titles, and bios as they appear on the current West Georgetown site.

Chief Executive Officer

R. Matthew George, D.C.

At our practice, we are committed to providing the highest quality medical and chiropractic care. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact me directly at drgeorge@anthonychiro.com.

Dr. George earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology from Marshall University in West Virginia in 2006. He continued his education at Parker College of Chiropractic, where he earned a second bachelor's degree in Anatomy and his Doctorate in Chiropractic Care in 2010.

In 2012, Dr. George joined Anthony Medical & Chiropractic Center and has grown with the company ever since. Today, he serves as an owning provider and the Chief Executive Officer, dedicated to enhancing patient care and outcomes.

A passionate advocate for health and wellness, Dr. George enjoys staying active with trail running and participating in community races and events. He also finds joy in golfing with family and friends. In addition to chiropractic care, Dr. George has a strong interest in computer science and is committed to leveraging technology to improve healthcare delivery and patient well-being.

When he's not working, Dr. George is a proud family man who treasures quality time with his wife and two children, creating lasting memories together. Outside of work and family, he has a deep love for concerts and live music, always seeking the next opportunity to enjoy the vibrant energy of a great performance.

Chiropractor

Matthew Kuo, D.C.

Dr. Matt earned his Bachelor of Arts in Athletic Training from Cedarville University in 2016 before receiving his Doctor of Chiropractic degree from Palmer College of Chiropractic in Davenport, Iowa, in 2020.

He has extensive experience caring for patients of all ages and has worked in a variety of practice settings, from large, multi-doctor family chiropractic clinics to launching new practices. Dr. Matt is skilled in several chiropractic techniques, including Activator, Diversified, Thompson Drop, Gonstead, and others, allowing him to tailor treatment plans to each patient's unique needs.

In addition, Dr. Matt is Webster Certified through the International Chiropractic Pediatric Association (ICPA), providing specialized care for pregnant patients and infants. He is passionate about helping patients identify the root cause of their concerns, empowering them with personalized treatment plans, and guiding them toward lasting health and healing.

In his free time, Dr. Matt enjoys time with his family, watching movies, reading, and experiencing new restaurants.

Nurse Provider

Sara Williamson, APRN, FNP-C

Sara Williamson, APRN, FNP-C graduated from Medcenter One College of Nursing in Bismarck, ND with a Bachelors of Nursing Science degree and began her nursing career as a commissioned officer in the United States Air Force. As a member of the Texas Air National Guard for 6 years and on active duty for 8 years, she proudly served her country.

Nurse Practitioner

An Huynh, APRN, FNP-C

An Huynh is a dedicated Family Nurse Practitioner with a passion for providing quality healthcare and a drive to make a positive impact on the lives of patients and their families. She obtained her Bachelor of Science in Nursing from UT Health Science Center in San Antonio.

After 8 years of working as an inpatient rehabilitation and acute care nurse, she became a Family Nurse Practitioner and received her Master of Nursing from UT Health Science Center in Houston.

In An's free time, she enjoys exploring the world through traveling, finding joy in discovering new cultures and cuisines. In addition to her love for food, An is also an avid boxing and fitness enthusiast. An can also be found spending quality time with family, friends, and her beloved German Shepherd dog named Lulu.
